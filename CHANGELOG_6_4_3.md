# Changelog 6.4.3

- Added an independent **Tempo de atividade** credit criterion worth 10 points.
- Age policy: `<1 year = 10%`, `1–3 years = 60%`, `>3 years = 100%` of the criterion.
- Redistributed the remaining 90 points across Legitimidade e cadastro (20), Estrutura e capacidade (25), Histórico público e risco (20), and Qualidade das evidências (25).
- Removed the accidental 50% missing-information factor; missing evidence now uses the configured 25% factor.
- Adjusted criterion formulas to improve differentiation between companies.
- Updated methodology text in the Streamlit UI.
